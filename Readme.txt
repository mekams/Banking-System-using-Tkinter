-Test cases are performed.

- It allows you to perform the following tasks:

1. Admin Login

2. Search for a particular customer to perform various banking operations on a customer bank account i.e. check balance, deposit or withdraw money, etc.

3. Deposit money into a customer account.

4. Withdraw money from a customer account.

5. Check the current balance for a customer.

6. View customer details e.g. name and address.

7. Update customer information e.g. name and address.

8. Delete a customer account i.e. remove a customer from the system.

9. Update admin's information i.e. name and address.

10. Print all customer's details.

11. Customers can have different types of bank accounts (e.g. current accounts and savings accounts). Accounts differ in their account name, interest rate overdraft limit, etc.

12. Store and load all customers’ data from and into a file.

13. Transferring funds/money from one account to another.

14. Add customer bank account with details.

15. Admins can request a management report. This should show the following information:

  a) Total number of customers in the system.

  b) The sum of all customers' money in their bank account.

  c) Using individual account balances, the report should calculate the sum of interest rate payable to all accounts for one year.

  d) Total amount of overdrafts currently taken by all customers.
